# Course Export

1. TOC
{:toc}


### Export Course

1. Once you have generated your course meshes and defined your hole and tee positions, you should see an Export Course button.

2. Save your course GLB file anywhere on your computer.

3. Wait for export to complete.

### Test your Course

You can test your freshly exported course GLB in the OpenGolfSim Desktop app. From the **File**{: .label } menu select **Test Course GLB**{: .label } and locate your exported course.
