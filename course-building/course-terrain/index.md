# Course Terrain

<img src="/assets/course-building/terrain-data.jpg" width="500" />

A well designed course starts with quality terrain. The first step in your course building journey will be choosing to find real-world terrain data and importing it into Unity or manually designing your own fictional course terrain using Unity's built-in terrain tools.
