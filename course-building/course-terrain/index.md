# Course Terrain

<img src="/assets/course-building/meshery/terrain.jpg" width="500" />

A well designed course starts with quality terrain. The first step in your course building journey will be choosing to find real-world terrain data or manually designing your own fictional course terrain using Meshery's terrain tools.
