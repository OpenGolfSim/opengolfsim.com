# Sky & Environment

You can customize the look and feel of your sky and course environment.

## Clouds

The default setting is for simple volumetric clouds you can customize with the density and color options.

## HDRIs

You can also load up to 8K HDRI/EXR images for your course sky. Find free HDRIs from various sites like:

- <a href="https://ambientcg.com/list?type=hdri" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AmbientCG</a>
- <a href="https://polyhaven.com/hdris" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Polyhaven</a>
