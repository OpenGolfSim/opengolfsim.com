# Satellite Layers

Generating a satellite layer is a recommended step before generating your SVG. It will be added to your SVG and used as a guide for drawing your course shapes.

{: .tip }
> You can preview the different satellite layers on the map before deciding which to generate for your course layers.

1. Click **Generate** to open the generate satellite dialog
2. Select the source you would like to capture
3. Click **Generate Satellite** and wait for the imagery to be captured.
