# Course Building Guide

At the time of writing. Building courses for OGS is still a new and experimental process and requires some existing knowledge of tools like Blender and Inkscape. We've built a custom course building app called [Course Meshery](/tools/course-meshery) that makes course building for OpenGolfSim FUSE as easy as possible.

Some helpful tips:

- **Work in small chunks.** It will be tempting to build out your full course right away, but the larger more complex the course, the more prone to issues. If you build a hole or two at a time and get them playable, it'll be easier to figure out issues as you go.

- **Be detail oriented.** Missing or skipping steps may not matter at first. But could compound into larger issues later.

- **Provide feedback.** Reach out on [Discord](/connect-with-us) to report bugs, discuss features, brainstorm or just say hi!


If you haven't already, make sure you complete the steps in the [Getting Started](/course-building/getting-started) section before diving into our course building guide.
