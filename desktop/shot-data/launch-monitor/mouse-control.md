# Mouse Control

The default launch monitor is our built-in mouse control. This lets you simply swipe down and then up to take shots. This is a useful mode for testing or just playing a round at your desk.

1. Go to the **Settings** > **Shot Data** section of OpenGolfSim.
2. Select **Change Launch Monitor**
3. Select **Mouse Control** from the list.

That's it. Launch a course or the range and swipe away.
