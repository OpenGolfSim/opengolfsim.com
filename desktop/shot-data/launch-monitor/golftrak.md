# GolfTrak

<a href="https://www.golftrak.app" target="_blank">GolfTrak</a> for iOS is a mobile application that transforms an iPhone into a portable, AI-powered golf launch monitor, using the camera to track shots without expensive hardware.

You can connect the GolfTrak app to send shots to OpenGolfSim using our built-in connector.

1. Go to the **Settings** > **Shot Data** section of OpenGolfSim.
2. Select **Change Launch Monitor**
3. Select **GolfTrak** from the list.
4. Enter your GolfTrak account email address when prompted.
5. OpenGolfSim should automatically discover and connect to the device.

### Troubleshooting

If OpenGolfSim can't locate the device, it usually indicates a network issue. Check that both devices are connected to the internet. Or reach out in our [Discord](/connect-with-us/) and we can try and help troubleshoot any issues.
